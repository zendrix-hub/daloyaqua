## The question
**Farm family income in Baleno is fragile to price and climate. What is the single biggest problem on our own land, in the words of the people who work it?**

Why now: a very strong El Niño is under way (PAGASA, 4 Nov 2026). Coconut yield is expected to dip from about mid-2027, and free insurance and PCA programs depend on registration. Sources: `docs/01-research/round7.md`.

## How Cowboy works
- Alone. Nothing is assigned to his brother or sister.
- One hour every **Sunday evening**.
- The study starts with the people who work the land, and it asks nothing of them but their knowledge.

## Who to learn from
- **The family:** the land's history and its records.
- **The bantay (caretaker; called the *taohan* in rounds 5–6):** the trees, the harvest, the drying and the sale.

## Finish line: the farm fact sheet
Filled in at the end of `farm-answers.md`.
1. What each parcel earned over the last 12 months, and the bantay's share.
2. Registry and insurance status: RSBSA, PCA's coconut farmer registry (NCFRS), whose name the land is under.
3. What the 2025 typhoons and the 2026 dry spell did to the trees.
4. Who buys the copra, at what price, with what deductions.
5. One sentence naming the biggest problem, in the words of mother, father and the bantay.

## Order of ideas
1. **Protect first:** registry and free insurance (registration is taken at the Baleno Municipal Agriculture Office, and the caretaker is eligible too; round 6), husk mulching around the best palms, a baseline of what the land earns.
2. **Earn more second:** copra sold on measured moisture and compared prices; whole or husked nuts if a buyer exists. At Christmas these are questions only, with no experiment and no meter purchase.
3. **Parked, not dropped:**
   - New copra products (VCO, sugar, coir, charcoal). Still one of the main problems to solve; revisit when there is a group of farmers, a buyer or capital.
   - Co-op compliance bookkeeping (rounds 3–4). Revisit when Cowboy has a contact inside a Baleno association.
   - Intercrops or livestock under the palms. Not before the 2027 rains.
   - Fishpond. Water source unchecked.

## Rules for AI agents
1. Call the founder **Cowboy**. Depth over hype; keep everything small.
2. Do not propose building software, new process or new documents. The work is conversations and one fact sheet.
3. Do not assign work to family members.
4. Cite a source URL for every factual claim and mark hypotheses 🧪. The refuted-claims list in `archive/2026-11-first-plan/HANDOFF.md` still applies: never present those as fact.
