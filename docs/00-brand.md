# DaloyAqua — Name & Meaning

**Daloy** (Filipino): *flow*, the way water moves. **Aqua** (Latin): *water*.

Water does not force its way. It flows steadily, finds the lowest path, and over time it shapes stone. That is the Zen idea behind the company, and the Atomic Habits idea too: small, constant flow beats big, rare effort.

## The four flows DaloyAqua exists to improve
| Flow | Today in Baleno | DaloyAqua's job |
|---|---|---|
| **Water** | Rainfed fields, soil/water limits, planned fishpond | Track and manage water for crops and ponds |
| **Data** | Farm sales and costs live in memory, so no local copra price history exists | Turn every sale and expense into data the family owns |
| **Cash** | Volatile copra prices, high input costs | Show the real profit per parcel; lead to better selling decisions |
| **Knowledge** | Agronomy (brother), accounting (sister), IT (Cowboy), lessons from Japan | Let the family's knowledge flow to Baleno farmers |

## One-line identity
> DaloyAqua helps Baleno farm families see and steer the flows of their farm: water, data, cash and knowledge.

## Brand principles
- **Flow over force:** tiny tools people actually use, not big systems they ignore.
- **Depth over hype:** every feature traces back to a verified farm problem.
- **Innovate by tinkering:** small experiments, logged in `docs/04-innovation.md`, kept or dropped based on evidence. Innovation is a habit, not a moment.
- **Local first:** built in Baleno, for Baleno, in the language farmers speak.
