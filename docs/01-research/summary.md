## 2. Users and adoption
- **Farm apps fail on usability:** PhilRice AgriDoc had 1,061 users, 74% of them college graduates, who found it tedious and English-only ✅. Advisory apps are widespread but little used, and community organizing bridges the gap ✅.
- **Bicol connectivity (Ligao, n=90):** 34% have reliable internet. 81–82% struggle to understand online information or lack the skills. Local-language content and audio are needed ✅ (not Masbate data).
- **Groups already exist in Baleno:** 3 DA-SAAD associations of 25 members each (poultry, corn/cassava, vegetables) and a BFAR fisherfolk group ✅.
- **→ Design rules:** spreadsheet before app · offline-first · someone helps enter the data · local language.

## 3. Who pays (the business)
*Direction parked on 2026-11-16 until there is a contact inside a Baleno association. The evidence stands.*
- **Farmers won't pay for software. Co-ops have to keep books.** Every CDA co-op over 1 year old must file a CAPR and audited FS each year to keep its **Certificate of Compliance**. Without it, the co-op gets no government programs, and it faces fines and revocation ✅.
- **What exists today is thin:** CDA provides rules (chart of accounts MC 2022-24, financial reporting framework MC 2022-25), an online-only portal (CAIS, which still needs hard copies), and occasional caravans. The only verified software, NATCCO eKoopBanker, reaches about 1.5% of co-ops and is built for credit co-ops ✅. → **A plausible gap that isn't proven yet** 🧪.
- **Closest analogue:** the JA Shin-Iwate bookkeeping service in Japan. Farmers pay from ¥35k/yr when a co-op they trust does the books ✅.
- **Budget signal:** CDA Region V spent ₱1.01M on co-op internal-control training (Nov 2025) ✅.
- **Later revenue:** pooled selling and market matching (Mayani earns from buyers and financing, not from farmers) ✅.
- **Value proposition 🧪:** year-round offline record-keeping → books in the CDA standard → FS ready for audit and a CAPR ready for CAIS → help with filing.

## 5. Japan lessons
JA bundles marketing, inputs, credit, insurance and farm guidance, and matches what members grow with what buyers want ✅. The JICA co-op loans of the 1990s showed that **weak co-op finances** are the failure point ✅. Strong books come first.

## 8. Rounds 5–6 — ground-level detail for Baleno
- **Official moisture rule:** 6% is the base with no deduction, 6.1–13.9% takes a sliding deduction, and 14% or more can be rejected (PCA AO 02 s.2003). *(high, round 5)*
- **How the buyer's weighing slip (*pesada*) works:** scale weight, less sack weight (*bawas sako*), less a quality deduction (*resiko* or *pasa*), times the quoted price. *(medium, round 5)*
- **Labour is paid by piece rate (*pakyawan*) or by crop share (*tersya*, traditionally one-third to the crew).** About 4.5–5 nuts make 1 kg of copra. *(medium, round 5)*
- **Baleno's three DA-SAAD associations are named:** Maluy-a Proper FA (corn and cassava, ₱532,750 allocation), Madancalan FA (poultry, ₱413,400) and Maan FFA (vegetables). They usually start as DOLE- or SEC-registered associations, not CDA co-ops. *(high, rounds 5–6)*
- **Masbate copra goes mainly to Legazpi**, with other buyers in Lucena and Cebu. *(medium-high, round 5)*
- **The coconut farmer registry (NCFRS) takes registrations at the Baleno Municipal Agriculture Office**, and farm workers and caretakers are eligible as well as owners. *(high, round 6)*
- **Baleno has no commercial bank;** cash moves through pawnshop agents in the Poblacion. Mobile signal is 4G in town and weak or absent in the hills. *(high and medium, round 6)*
- **All three co-op federation software packages target credit co-ops**, which supports the gap noted in section 3. *(medium, round 5)*

## 8b. Round 7 — climate, earning more, Japan in depth
**Climate**
- A **very strong El Niño** is present (PAGASA, 4 Nov 2026; NOAA, 12 Nov 2026), peaking around the end of 2026 and lasting into the first half of 2027. Masbate is forecast in dry spell. *(high)*
- **Rice and corn carry the immediate risk.** Coconut shows drought about 13 months later, so the dip is expected from mid-2027; a 10–20% fall is a planning number, not a measured one. *(lag: high; size: low)*
- **Typhoons:** Opong made landfall in Masbate on 26 Sep 2025; Baleno was under a wind signal during Uwan in Nov 2025. **No Masbate coconut damage figures exist** in any source found.
- **Safety nets depend on registration.** PCIC coconut cover is free for farmers in PCA's registry (NCFRS) and appears to cover typhoon and pest, not drought. Rice and corn cover is free for RSBSA-listed farmers. *(medium)*
- **Cheapest protection:** husk mulching around palms (+15–20% nuts in Sri Lankan trials). Hold replanting and new intercrops until the 2027 rains. *(medium)*

**Earning more from the same coconuts**
- **Selling on better terms beats processing** for one person with little capital: copra on measured moisture and compared prices, then whole or husked nuts if a buyer exists. *(medium)*
- **Processing is a poor fit now:** VCO, coconut sugar, coir, coconut water and desiccated coconut need daily labour, a co-op of 100+ members, or FDA licensing and outside markets. *(medium)*
- **No mill, coir plant or shared processing facility was found in Masbate.** The only Masbate copra price found is ₱52.60/kg (Feb 2025), about 6% under the Bicol average.

**Japan in depth**
- **JA cannot be copied whole:** its farmer services are paid for by banking and insurance on non-farmer money. *(medium)*
- **Records are the entry ticket** to insurance and shipment programmes, and paper is still the default (17% of farms used data in 2020). *(medium-high)*
- **Farm processing raised sales for about three-quarters of certified operators but profit for about half.** *(medium)*
- **One transfer worked:** JICA's Cambodia co-op project, on standard bookkeeping formats and simple businesses. *(high, JICA's own evaluation)*
