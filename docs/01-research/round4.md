# Research Round 4 — Co-op Bookkeeping: What Exists Today (2026-11-05)

Method: deep-research workflow covering 5 angles and 21 sources. 25 claims verified with 3 adversarial votes each: **14 confirmed, 11 rejected.** That's a high rejection rate, so read the confidence labels.
Hypothesis tested: *small rural farmer co-ops struggle to produce the CDA annual report (CAPR) and financial statements needed for a Certificate of Compliance (COC), and nothing serves them well* (I-004).

## Verdict
**Plausible, under-served gap, but not proven.** What CDA gives co-ops for free is **rules, templates and an online-only portal, not bookkeeping help.** The only co-op software we could verify reaches about 1.5% of co-ops and is built for credit operations. CDA itself says online filing is hard for "digitally deprived localities", and Region V runs in-person caravans for non-compliant co-ops. **Still missing:** non-compliance figures for Bicol, prices (auditor, bookkeeper), and evidence that co-ops would pay. Fieldwork has to answer these (G3, G5).

## Findings
1. **Compliance is demanding (MC 2025-02, Feb 2025). *(medium, 2-1)*** Within 120 days of year-end, every co-op except CSF co-ops files a COC package: the CAPR, an **8-part set of financial statements audited by a CDA-accredited external auditor**, a social audit, a performance audit, other reports, and a ₱100 fee. The circular's text contains **no** audit exemption for micro co-ops.
   - https://cda.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/MC-2025-02-with-annexes.pdf
2. **⚠️ Rules conflict, unresolved. *(medium)*** The older MC 2019-04 let micro co-ops (≤₱3M assets) be audited by **their own or their federation's Audit Committee**. The 2025 CDA reminders list only "under 1 year: signed FS / over 1 year: externally audited FS". **Whether Baleno co-ops must pay an external auditor is unknown.** Ask CDA Region V. Co-ops registered under 1 year file unaudited FS and get their first COC automatically.
   - https://cda.gov.ph/memorandum-circulars/mc-2019-04-guidelines-governing-the-submission-of-required-reports-for-micro-cooperatives
   - https://sites.google.com/cda.gov.ph/coop-reminders/annual-reports
3. **Non-compliance is costly. *(high)*** ₱100/day fine on officers (applied explicitly to small, medium and large co-ops, so micro co-ops may be exempt). No COC → a Show Cause Order in July → revocation proceedings (RA 9520 Art. 53). Micro co-ops get tagged "NON-COMPLIANT".
   - https://cda.gov.ph/region-3/reminder-letter-on-the-submission-of-mandatory-reports-for-fy-2025/
4. **CDA provides standards, not software or labor. *(high)*** Revised Standard Chart of Accounts (MC 2022-24) and Amended Financial Reporting Framework for co-ops (MC 2022-25), Sept 2022, plus seminars. These cover agricultural and multipurpose co-ops.
   - https://cda.gov.ph/region-ncr/mandaluyong-city-seminar-on-the-amended-philippines-financial-reporting-framework-for-cooperatives-and-the-revised-standard-chart-of-accounts/
   - → **DaloyAqua's chart of accounts should follow MC 2022-24** (task for the sister).
5. **Filing is online-only through CAIS** (cs.cda.gov.ph/cais, replaced CAPRIS from CY2023), **and original hard copies are still required.** A rural co-op needs internet access *and* a way to deliver paper. *(high, 2-1)*
6. **CDA admits that online filing hurt compliance** in "digitally deprived localities" (FY2022 report; the reporting count dropped, partly because of COVID). *(medium; old, self-reported)*
   - https://cda.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/BAR-1-PhysicalofOperation-FY-2023-CDA.pdf
7. **Non-compliance is a recognized problem in Bicol.** CDA Region V held a Compliance Caravan in Sorsogon (Oct 2024, 168+ attendees). Invited external auditors gave bookkeeping help, and CDA explained the **Amnesty Program** for non-compliant or dormant co-ops. The response was in-person events plus private auditors. *(medium)*
   - https://cda.gov.ph/region-5/two-day-special-compliance-caravan-and-handholding-activity-held-for-non-compliant-cooperatives-in-province-of-sorsogon/
8. **NATCCO eKoopBanker is the only co-op software verified.** It's at 623 sites / 269 co-ops (about 1.5% of roughly 18–20k co-ops), it's core banking for credit co-ops, it generates FS, and its price isn't published. *(medium; vendor's own claims)*
   - https://natcco.coop/services/support-services/digital-solutions/

## Competitive landscape
| Alternative | Provider | Cost | Target | Gap for small rural farmer co-ops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard chart of accounts + financial reporting framework | CDA | Free | Agri/multipurpose co-ops | Rules only, no tool and no labor |
| CAIS portal | CDA | Free (₱100 COC) | All co-ops (filing) | Online-only + hard copies; does no bookkeeping |
| Compliance caravans | CDA V + external auditors | Free to attend | Non-compliant co-ops | Occasional, not ongoing |
| eKoopBanker | NATCCO | Not published | Credit co-ops | Built for credit/banking; no evidence of farmer co-op use |
| External auditor / CPA | Private | **Unknown** | Co-ops >1 year | Cost and availability in Masbate unknown |
| Audit Committee audit | Co-op / federation | Internal | Micro co-ops (if still allowed) | Depends on volunteer skill; may be superseded |
| Generic cloud accounting, DA/PCA/ATI programs, JA/India/SEA analogues | — | **Not verified** | — | Needs more research |

## Correction to Round 3
Round 3 (A1) said micro co-ops "may be audited by their own Audit Committee." That rests on MC 2019-04, and **whether it still applies under MC 2025-02 is now uncertain** (finding 2).

## What this changes for DaloyAqua
- **The value proposition gets sharper:** "Offline record-keeping during the year → CDA-standard (MC 2022-24) books → audit-ready FS + CAIS-ready CAPR → we help with filing." That's year-round help, compared with CDA's one-off caravans.
- **A partnership angle:** external auditors are the bottleneck and a partner (they audit what we prepare). CDA Region V caravans are a channel.
- **The Amnesty Program** for non-compliant or dormant co-ops could be a first wedge 🧪.
- **Risk:** if Baleno groups are DOLE/SEC/LGU-registered, not CDA, the COC pain doesn't apply. G3 question 2 checks this.
