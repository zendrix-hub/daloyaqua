# Research Round 3 — Who Pays & Funding (2026-11-05)

Method: deep-research workflow covering 5 angles and 20 sources. 25 claims verified with 3 adversarial votes each: 21 confirmed, 4 rejected. After merging they make 11 findings.

> ⚠️ **Correction to Round 2 (A4):** the claim that CFIDP/CocoFarmCoop *funds a computerized accounting system and FS/audit subsidies* was **refuted (1-2)** this round. CFIDP is **not** a confirmed software buyer. What did survive: market matching (including digital) and help with the coconut registry.

## A. Who pays — co-op compliance (strongest lead)
1. **Co-op compliance requires bookkeeping every year. *(high)*** Every CDA cooperative older than 1 year must file an annual report (CAPR) with audited financial statements to get a **Certificate of Compliance (COC)**. **Without a COC, the co-op can't be endorsed for CDA or other agency programs.** Micro co-ops (≤₱3M assets) may be audited by their own Audit Committee instead of a paid CPA. ⚠️ *Round 4: this rests on MC 2019-04, and it's uncertain whether it still applies under MC 2025-02. Verify with CDA Region V.*
   - https://cda.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/MC-2025-02-with-annexes.pdf
   - https://sites.google.com/cda.gov.ph/coop-reminders/annual-reports
   - https://cda.gov.ph/memorandum-circulars/mc-2019-04-guidelines-governing-the-submission-of-required-reports-for-micro-cooperatives
2. **CFIDP/CocoFarmCoop components** include market matching (traditional, online or digital) and help registering farmers in the National Coconut Farmers Registry (NCFRS). Registry membership is the gateway to CFIDP benefits. *(medium; MIMAROPA 2023, Bicol not confirmed)*
   - https://cda.gov.ph/region-4b/cda-pca-partnership-implementation-on-mimaropa-region-coconut-farmers-organization-and-development-through-cooperatives-cocofarmcoop
3. **CDA Region V spends on coconut co-op capacity building.** A Nov 2025 procurement notice had a ₱1,011,500 budget for "Business Management and **Internal Control and Inventory System** for Coconut Farmer Cooperatives". It bought training services, not software, but shows a budget and a way in. *(high)*
   - https://cda.gov.ph/region-5/abc-1011500-00-entrepreneurial-and-business-management-and-internal-control-and-inventory-system-for-coconut-farmer-cooperatives-cfcs/

## B. Founder funding (non-dilutive)
4. **DA Young Farmers Challenge (Kabataang Agribiz) is the most realistic grant. *(medium)*** In 2026, Start-Up winners got ₱300k and Up-Scale winners got ₱500k. There is a **Digital Agriculture** subcategory. Applicants must be 18–30 and natural-born Filipino. Entry runs provincial → regional (V) → national.
   - https://ptvnews.ph/ani-at-kita-da-opens-agri-biz-program-for-youth-sector-prize-up-to-p500-k/ (+ 2026 coverage: Daily Guardian, Tribune, Manila Bulletin)
5. **ACPC KAYA: up to ₱500k at 0% interest, no collateral, 5 years to repay** (lender service fee ≤3.5%). For agri-fishery entrepreneurs aged 18–30 with an agri, ATI or TESDA background who are in RSBSA. Needs a simple business plan. Fits the **brother's farm enterprise**, not the software. *(high)*
   - https://acpc.gov.ph/kapital-access-for-young-agripreneurs-kaya/
6. **DICT Startup Grant Fund: about ₱500k–1M, equity-free, for proof of concept to MVP.** Requires a **registered PH business** and a Startup Number. No 2025–26 call was found. *(low)* The DOST SGF requires 1–5 years of DTI/SEC registration.
   - https://assistance.ph/dict-startup-grant-fund-sgf-program/

## C. Not a fit for now
7. **DTI Startup Venture Fund:** only about 3% deployed. It only co-invests with private VCs, from seed to Series B (₱5–50M tickets). S3 at the earliest. *(medium)* — https://context.ph/?p=49299
8. **JICA Biz** funds **Japanese companies**, not Filipino startups (e.g. Kuwasato mulberry tea with Pampanga farmers). A route for DaloyAqua would be to **partner with a Japanese company**. *(medium)*
   - https://www.jica.go.jp/activities/schemes/priv_partner/subject/regions/asia/philippine.html
   - The claim that the JICA NINJA accelerator is still running was refuted (0-3).

## D. International and models
9. **Grow Asia Innovation Challenge 2025** (IFAD + Korea MAFRA, with PPSA and Villgro PH) focused on **coconut**, cacao and coffee in the Philippines. The 2026 cycle is unconfirmed. *(medium)*
   - https://www.dls.growasia.org/post/grow-asia-innovation-challenge-2025-top-10-innovators-showcase
10. **Mayani** (closest PH comparison) earns from **buyers, the supply chain, inputs and financing**, not from farmers paying for software. It works with about 144k organized smallholders. GCash uses Mayani's supply-chain data for co-op loans. *(medium)*
    - https://theindependentinvestor.ph/agritech-startup-mayani-bridges-agri-credit-gap-with-gcash-partnership/

## E. Eligibility blockers (our synthesis, low confidence)
- **Business registration** is needed for DICT/DOST SGF and SVF → S0/S1 must use competitions and loans to individuals.
- **Age 18–30 + citizenship** for YFC and KAYA → confirm each sibling's age.
- **Residency:** KAYA needs a local lender and RSBSA, and YFC has regional rounds → **the siblings in the Philippines should be the named applicants** while Cowboy is in Japan.
- **JICA Biz** needs a Japanese company as the proposer.
- **Co-op routes** fund the co-op, not DaloyAqua → DaloyAqua is a **service provider** to the co-op.
