## B. DA-ACPC Lending Conduits for Masbate (KAYA & SURE)

3. **How KAYA and SURE loans are actually accessed in Masbate. *(high)***
   - **Online entry:** Applications begin at **`https://acpcaccess.ph`**, followed by simple business plan evaluation.
   - **Frontline Partner Lending Conduits (PLCs):** In the Bicol Region, ACPC disburses loans through accredited rural and cooperative banks. The prominent regional partner bank serving Bicol is the **Rural Bank of Guinobatan, Inc. (RBGI)**, along with accredited local cooperative financial institutions.
   - **Local Agency Contact:** The program is administered through the **DA Regional Field Office No. 5 – Agribusiness and Marketing Assistance Division (DA-RFO 5 AMAD)** in coordination with the Provincial Agriculture Office of Masbate.
   - https://acpc.gov.ph
   - **Action for Brother:** Brother does not need to visit an ACPC head office. He can register his agribusiness profile at `acpcaccess.ph` and inquire with DA RFO 5 AMAD regarding the active Masbate PLC branch.

## C. PCA NCFRS Coconut Registration in Baleno

4. **The National Coconut Farmers Registry System (NCFRS) mechanism. *(high)***
   Under RA 11524 (Coconut Farmers and Industry Trust Fund Act), the NCFRS is the master database for all CFIDP interventions:
   - **Who qualifies:** Farm owner-tillers, growers (≥0.5 ha, min 20 trees), tenants, and **farm workers / laborers (*taohan*)**. That means our family's *taohan* who cooks and hauls copra is **legally eligible to be registered in NCFRS**.
   - **Registration hub in Baleno:** Handled directly through the **Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) Baleno** during regular intake or mobile registration caravans, as well as the PCA Provincial Office in Masbate City.
   - **Direct benefits:** Free hybrid coconut seedlings (143 seedlings/ha), fertilizer subsidies, and free **Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC)** coconut tree mortality insurance.
   - https://pca.gov.ph / https://da.gov.ph
   - **Action for Brother at Baleno MAO (`G4`):** Ask the Municipal Agriculturist for the NCFRS form for the family farm parcels and check if our *taohan* is enrolled.

## D. Baleno Cash and Telecommunications Infrastructure

5. **Financial rails: No commercial banks, remittance cash dominance. *(high)***
   - There are **no commercial bank branches** in Baleno (commercial banks like Landbank, DBP, BDO are located in Masbate City, ~25 km away via provincial road).
   - Cash transactions, remittances, and micro-financial services in Baleno run through **Cebuana Lhuillier Pawnshop** and **Palawan Pawnshop** agents located in **Poblacion, Baleno**.
   - Traders pay farm sales in physical cash. When cash needs to be transferred or mobile wallets topped up, farmers rely on over-the-counter cash-in/cash-out at pawnshop counters.
6. **Cellular signal: Severe terrain attenuation outside Poblacion. *(medium)***
   - **Smart/TNT** and **Globe** operate 4G/LTE cellular towers providing coverage in Poblacion and accessible coastal corridors.
   - However, signals drop sharply to 2G/3G or dead zones in interior hilly barangays due to terrain obstruction. **DITO** footprint is concentrated in Masbate City and has negligible penetration in rural Baleno.
   - **System confirmation:** This confirms that **any future mobile tool (Spec 002 / S1) must be strictly offline-first**. Real-time cloud-only apps will fail in the farm parcels.
