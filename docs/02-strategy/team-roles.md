# Team & Roles

Three siblings, three disciplines. Together they cover the AI + agriculture + accounting goal in Cowboy's VGMO.

| Person | Discipline | Role now (S0–S1) | Future role (S2+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cowboy** | IT | Product owner and system owner. Writes specs, builds tools, reviews data weekly (from Cebu, later from Japan) | CEO / CTO |
| **Brother** | Registered agriculturist | Domain owner for the farm: defines parcels and crops, validates agronomy, ideally logs on-site in Baleno | Head of Agronomy / field operations, link to DA, BFAR and farmer associations |
| **Sister** | Management accounting | Finance owner: chart of accounts, cost categories, monthly profit-per-parcel review | CFO: unit economics, pricing, grants, bookkeeping |

## Operating rhythm
- **Daily:** on-site logger records each sale and expense (2-minute rule).
- **Weekly:** Cowboy reviews the data and fixes the tool, one small improvement per week.
- **Monthly:** 15-minute family call to go over profit per parcel, the copra price trend, and one decision.
- **Per stage gate:** all three agree that the gate in `docs/03-sdlc.md` was met before the next stage starts.

## Decision rules
- Agronomy questions: brother decides. Money and accounting questions: sister decides. Tech and product questions: Cowboy decides.
- Anything that spends more than an agreed amount (set by sister) needs all three.
