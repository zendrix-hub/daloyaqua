# Team & Roles

Three siblings, three disciplines. Together they cover the AI + agriculture + accounting goal in Cowboy's VGMO.

| Person | Discipline | Role now (S0–S1) | Future role (S2+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cowboy** | IT | Product owner and system owner. Writes specs, builds tools, reviews data weekly (from Cebu, later from Japan) | CEO / CTO |
| **Mother** | Farm treasury & turnover reviewer | Receives the receipt (*pesada*) and cash from the *taohan* after each copra sale, calculates and reviews the sale, and allocates the proceeds between our family and the *taohan* | Family farm advisor & trust anchor in Baleno |
| **Brother** | Registered agriculturist | Domain owner for the farm: defines parcels and crops, validates agronomy, logs (or assists Mother in logging) on-site in Baleno | Head of Agronomy / field operations, link to DA, BFAR and farmer associations |
| **Sister** | Management accounting | Finance owner: chart of accounts (`MC 2022-24`), cost categories, monthly profit-per-parcel review | CFO: unit economics, pricing, grants, bookkeeping |

## Operating rhythm
- **Every sale turnover:** when the *taohan* turns over the receipt (*pesada*) and cash to Mother, Mother/brother logs one row on `Sales` (`Taohan Share` vs `Our Family Share` calculated automatically in under 2 minutes). Standalone expenses go on `Costs`.
- **Weekly:** Cowboy reviews the data and fixes the tool, one small improvement per week.
- **Monthly:** 15-minute family call to go over profit per parcel, the copra price trend, and one decision.
- **Per stage gate:** all three agree that the gate in `docs/03-sdlc.md` was met before the next stage starts.

## Decision rules
- Agronomy questions: brother decides. Money and accounting questions: sister decides. Tech and product questions: Cowboy decides.
- Anything that spends more than an agreed amount (set by sister) needs all three.
