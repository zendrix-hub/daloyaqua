# Farm answers (blank template)

> This repo is public. Copy this file somewhere outside the repo before filling it in. Never upload a filled copy.

Questions for mother, father and the bantay, each with its reason and a space for the answer. Write answers the same day, in whatever language they were given. Ask about what actually happened, not what someone thinks should happen.

How to open: "I'm worried about El Niño and want to understand how our farm works."

---

**A1. What papers do we have for the land (a title or a tax declaration), and whose name is on them? Do we have any ID, stub or form from the municipal agriculture office or from PCA?** ★ must-get
*Why: an agriculture-office stub means the farmer registry (RSBSA); a PCA form means the coconut farmer registry (NCFRS). These decide whether free insurance and PCA programs are open to the family. Registration is taken at the Baleno Municipal Agriculture Office, and the caretaker can register too (round 6).*

**A2. How is the copra money split with the bantay, and who pays for transport and sacks?** ★ must-get
*Why: shows who gains from any improvement. If there is more than one parcel or bantay, ask for each.*

**A3. At the last copra sale, in this order: how many kilos? What price per kilo? What was taken off? How much did you receive after the bantay's share?** ★ must-get
*Why: the first real row for the logbook baseline. Note the date and which parcel it came from. On the buyer's weighing slip (pesada), "taken off" is usually sack weight (bawas sako) plus a quality deduction (resiko or pasa); see round 5.*

## C. The bantay — Christmas break, on the land

Walk the parcels and let them show you.

*Why: tree count and typhoon damage from the only person who knows.*

**C2. Walk me through the last harvest: how many nuts, how many days, how many people?**

**C3. How do you dry the copra, for how long, and what do you burn?**
*Why: shows whether drying is the weak point and whether shells and husks are already used.*

**C4. How do you know it's dry enough?**
