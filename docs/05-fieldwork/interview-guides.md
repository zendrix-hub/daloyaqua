# Fieldwork Interview Guides

Phase 0 (Research) field validation. Each guide maps to a hypothesis in the `HANDOFF.md` checklist (F1–F7) and an idea in `docs/04-innovation.md`.

## The 6 rules (from *The Mom Test*, Rob Fitzpatrick)
1. **Ask about their life, not our idea.** Don't pitch DaloyAqua until the end, if at all.
2. **Ask about the past, not the future.** "What did you do last time?" beats "Would you use…?" People are polite about the future and honest about the past.
3. **Ask for specifics and numbers.** "How much did it cost?" "When was the last time?" "Who did it?"
4. **Listen for money, time and workarounds.** If they already spend money or time fixing the problem, it's real. If they've never tried to fix it, it may not hurt enough.
5. **Compliments are not data.** "Ganda ng idea!" ("great idea!") tells you nothing. Turn it back: "Salamat po. Paano n'yo po ito ginagawa ngayon?" ("Thank you. How do you do this today?")
6. **Ask for a commitment at the end.** Their time, an introduction, a document, or a trial. A real "yes" costs them something.

## Ethics & etiquette
- Introduce yourself honestly: family farm, research, no sale today.
- Ask before recording or taking photos. Don't record officials without explicit consent.
- **Don't promise** grants, prices, or a free app.
- Keep names private in the repo. Use codes like `BUYER-01`, `ASSOC-01`.
- Bring something small (merienda) for association meetings. Respect their time: 20–30 minutes.

Questions are in English with Filipino versions. **Brother: adapt them to Masbateño** when you speak.

## G1 — On-site logger commitment at Mother's turnover (F1)
**Who:** Mother + Brother · **By:** Cowboy (call) · **Time:** 10 minutes · **Hypothesis:** every time the *taohan* turns over a copra sale to Mother, Mother or Brother will log that turnover row in `Sales` (and any standalone farm expenses in `Costs`) the same day.

1. "Last time the *taohan* sold copra and turned the money over to Mama, how did Mama calculate the split between our share and the *taohan*'s share?"
2. "Did the *taohan* bring a written receipt (*pesada*) from the buyer? Where is it kept now?"
3. "Show the `Sales` sheet (2 minutes): look at how entering `Qty (kg)`, `Price`, `Transport`, and `Taohan Share (₱)` automatically calculates `Net (₱)` and `Our Family Share (₱)`. Let's type the last sale Mama remembers right now." → **Time it.**
4. **Commitment:** "Every time the *taohan* turns over a sale to Mama, can Mama or Brother log that one row on `Sales` (plus any standalone expenses on `Costs`) for the next 30 days? I'll check every Sunday."

**Signal:** ✅ logs a turnover row in ≤2 minutes and says yes to 30 days. ❌ Hesitates → let Mama/Brother send a photo of the *pesada* and voice note on Messenger right after the turnover, and Cowboy logs it (I-005).

## G2 — Copra buyer & *Taohan* sale check (F2, I-001, I-003)
**Who:** First, ask our *taohan* who cooks, transports, and bargains the copra; then Brother accompanies the *taohan* (or visits the buyer) on the next 1–3 sales in Baleno · **By:** Brother (and Mother at turnover) · **Time:** 10–15 minutes at a sale or turnover · **Hypothesis:** moisture is judged by eye, and deductions (*bawas sako* + *resiko*) are 14–22%.

Ask casually during or after a real sale. This is a conversation, not an audit.
1. "How do you check the moisture, by look and feel or with a meter?" *(Paano n'yo po sinusukat ang moisture: tingin/hawak o may metro?)*
2. "How much did you deduct today, and why?" → **Write down the % and the gross vs net kg.**
3. "Where does the price you pay come from? Do you get a daily price from someone?"
4. "Who do you sell to: another trader, a mill in Masbate City, or outside the province?" → map the layers.
5. "Do you pay more for drier copra or bigger volume? How much more?"
6. "When the price drops, how do you hear about it?"

**Record:** method, deduction %, gross/net kg, price/kg, buyer type, their buyer. Enter it in the logbook (fields from spec 003).
**Signal:** ✅ visual grading + deduction ≥10% confirms I-001. A volume premium supports pooled selling (S3).
**Don't:** accuse them of cheating. Traders are future partners.

## G3 — Association officer (F3, I-004) ⭐ top priority
**Who:** Chair, treasurer or bookkeeper of a Baleno farmer association or co-op — e.g. the 3 verified DA-SAAD Phase 2 groups in Baleno (**Maluy-a Proper Farmers Association** in Brgy. Obongan Diot [corn/cassava, already doing profit-sharing & MAED bookkeeping training], **Madancalan Farmers Association** in Brgy. Madangcalan [poultry], or **Maan Farmers and Fisherfolks Association** in Brgy. Polot [vegetables]; see `docs/01-research/round5.md` C6–C7), or a coconut co-op · **By:** Sister (accounting knowledge helps) · **Time:** 25–30 minutes · **Hypothesis:** they struggle to produce their annual report and financial statements, and would pay for help or try it.

**Warm-up**
1. "Tell me about your association: how many members, what do you produce, how long have you been organized?"
2. "Are you registered with CDA, DOLE, SEC, or DA only?" → *Only CDA co-ops need the COC/CAPR. Associations registered elsewhere have different requirements. Note which.*

**Current behavior (the core)**
3. "Who keeps your books today? Is that person paid?" *(Sino po ang nag-aasikaso ng libro/records ninyo? May bayad po ba?)*
4. "Walk me through the last time you prepared your annual report (CAPR) or financial statements. Who did it, how long did it take, what was hard?"
5. "How much did it cost you last year, including the bookkeeper, auditor/CPA, travel to the CDA office, photocopies?" → **Get a peso number.**
5a. "Who audited your financial statements last time: an outside auditor, or your own Audit Committee?" → *rules may have changed in 2025 (round 4)*
5b. "How did you submit to CAIS online? Who did the encoding, where did you get internet, how did you send the hard copies?"
6. "Have you ever been late, or missed a report? What happened? Did you lose a program or a grant?"
7. "What records do you keep for DA-SAAD or other programs? Is it the same work, done again?"
8. "What do you use: a notebook, Excel, software, a federation service?" → *Compare with the landscape table in `docs/01-research/round4.md`.*
9. "If your bookkeeper left tomorrow, what would happen?"

**Money and workarounds**
10. "Have you ever paid someone or tried a tool to make this easier? What happened?"
11. "If you could fix one thing about your association's records, what would it be?"

**Commitment (choose one)**
12. "Could I see a sample of your ledger or last report, with names hidden?" or "Could we help you prepare next year's report as a free trial?" or "Who else should I talk to?"

**Record:** registration type, bookkeeper (paid or volunteer), annual compliance cost in ₱, hours, problems, current tools, whether they committed.
**Signal:** ✅ a real cost (₱ or many hours) + a past penalty or near miss + agrees to share a sample or try → I-004 moves to a spec. ❌ "No problem, our treasurer handles it" from 2 or more associations → park I-004 or rethink who pays.

## G4 — LGU Baleno Municipal Agriculture Office (F4)
**Who:** Municipal Agriculturist or an Agricultural Technologist · **By:** Brother (as a registered agriculturist, a peer) · **Time:** 20 minutes · **Hypothesis:** there's budget or interest in association capacity building, records or a price board.

1. "Which farmer associations and co-ops are active in Baleno now? Which are strongest and weakest?"
2. "What support did the MAO give associations last year: training, inputs, record-keeping help?"
3. "How do you collect farmgate prices today? Who uses that data?" *(Paano po kinokolekta ang farmgate prices dito?)*
4. "What are the most common reasons associations become inactive?"
5. "Is any budget planned for association capacity building or digital tools? Through which program (LGU, DA, SAAD, PCA)?"
6. "Who in the province handles CFIDP or PCA coconut programs?"

**Commitment:** an introduction to 1–2 association officers for G3, or a copy of the farmer association list.
**Signal:** ✅ names weak associations + records problems + a budget line or program. ❌ "No budget, no interest" → S2 sponsor must come from elsewhere.

## G5 — CDA Region V / PCA Masbate (F5)
**Who:** CDA Region V extension office (or the Masbate cooperative development officer) · PCA Masbate provincial office · **By:** Brother or sister, by phone or visit · **Time:** 15 minutes · **Hypothesis:** CocoFarmCoop/CFIDP is active in Masbate, and providers can take part.

**CDA**
1. "How many co-ops in Masbate, or Baleno, are compliant vs delinquent with their CAPR/COC?" → *a number if possible.*
2. "What are the most common reasons small co-ops fail to submit?"
3. "What help does CDA give micro co-ops on bookkeeping: templates, training, software?"
3a. "For CY2025 reports, can a micro co-op (≤₱3M assets) still be audited by its own or its federation's Audit Committee, or must it hire an accredited external auditor? How much does an external audit usually cost in Masbate?" ⭐ *the most important open rule (round 4)*
3b. "Is the Amnesty Program open to dormant or non-compliant co-ops in Masbate? How many have applied?"
3c. "Will there be a compliance caravan in Masbate? Could a local bookkeeping helper join?"
4. "Is the CDA–PCA CocoFarmCoop program active in Masbate? Which co-ops?"
5. "Can private providers be accredited or contracted for co-op bookkeeping training or systems?"

**PCA**
6. "How many Baleno farmers are in the National Coconut Farmers Registry (NCFRS)? How do farmers register?"
7. "Which CFIDP components are open to Masbate farmers now, especially market matching?"

**Commitment:** contact person, the list of coconut co-ops in Masbate, or the CFIDP schedule.

## G6 — Neighbor coconut farmer (I-003 price board, optional)
**Who:** 2–3 neighbors who sell copra · **By:** Brother or a parent · **Time:** 10 minutes
1. "When did you last sell copra? To whom, at what price, how many kilos?"
2. "How did you decide where to sell and when?"
3. "How do you find out the price before you sell?"
4. "Have you ever found out later that someone got a better price? What did you do?"
5. **Commitment:** "Would you share your sale price with us after each sale for a month? We'll share ours too."

**Signal:** ✅ ≥5 farmers agree to swap prices → run the I-003 experiment.
