# Funding & Payer Roadmap (v1, 2026-11-05)

Based on `docs/01-research/round3.md`. ✅ = verified · 🧪 = our inference, check by direct contact.

## Core insight
**Farmers won't pay for software. Co-ops have to keep books.** Every co-op needs annual financial statements to keep its Certificate of Compliance, and without that certificate it can't get government programs ✅. DaloyAqua's paying customer is the **co-op, as a bookkeeping and compliance service** (the JA bookkeeping-service model from round 2). Later revenue can come from buyers through pooled selling (the Mayani model ✅).
